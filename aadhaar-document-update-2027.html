Aadhaar Document Update 2027: Online Process, Documents and Fees

By TechNexiss Editorial Desk

If your Aadhaar documents are old, updating supporting documents can help keep your record current. This guide explains what to prepare, how the online process works and where to verify the latest rules.

Important: Fees, deadlines and accepted documents can change. Always verify the latest information on the official UIDAI/MyAadhaar portal before submitting a request.

What is Aadhaar document update?

Document update means submitting current identity or address proof through the Aadhaar update service. It is not the same as every type of demographic correction, so check the exact service you need before submitting documents.

What do you need?

  • Aadhaar number
  • Registered mobile number for online OTP authentication
  • A clear, valid supporting document
  • Access to the official MyAadhaar portal

How to update documents online

  1. Open the official MyAadhaar portal.
  2. Sign in using Aadhaar number and OTP.
  3. Select the document update service available to you.
  4. Read UIDAI's document requirements.
  5. Upload a clear, readable document.
  6. Submit the request and save the acknowledgement/reference details.
  7. Check the request status through the official portal when available.

Which documents are accepted?

Accepted Proof of Identity and Proof of Address documents depend on UIDAI's current rules and the service being used. Check the official UIDAI list rather than relying on an unofficial list.

Can document update change your name or date of birth?

Not necessarily. Document submission and demographic correction are separate services. If you need to change a particular detail, follow UIDAI's instructions for that specific update.

What if your mobile number is not registered?

Online services use the registered mobile number for OTP authentication. If online authentication is not possible, an Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre may be more suitable.

Safety tips

  • Use only official UIDAI/MyAadhaar websites.
  • Never share your OTP.
  • Do not enter Aadhaar details on unknown links.
  • Verify fees before paying anyone.
  • Keep your acknowledgement/reference details.
