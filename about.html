India's most trusted digital portal for Government schemes, Jobs, Results, Weather & Technology news

Who We Are

TechNexiss is India's premier digital news and information portal, founded with one mission — to bridge the information gap between government initiatives and common Indian citizens. We provide accurate, verified, and easy-to-understand content on Sarkari Yojana, Government Jobs, Competitive Exam Results, Admit Cards, Weather Updates, and Technology news — all in one place.

Since our inception, TechNexiss has grown into a trusted source for millions of Indians who rely on us daily to stay updated about the latest government schemes, job notifications, exam results, and important news that directly affects their lives.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: Make government information accessible to every Indian citizen — in simple Hindi and English, without confusion or complexity.

We believe that every citizen deserves to know about the benefits, schemes, and opportunities available to them by the government. Whether it's PM Kisan installments, SSC job notifications, UPSC results, or IMD weather alerts — we deliver it first, accurately.

✅ Our Core Values

  • Accuracy First: All content verified from official government sources (gov.in portals)
  • Transparency: We clearly disclose our sources, authors, and editorial process
  • Independence: Editorial content is never influenced by advertisers
  • Accessibility: We write in Hindi and English for maximum reach
  • Timeliness: Breaking government news published within hours of official announcement

Trust & Verification

Every article published on TechNexiss goes through our editorial verification process:

  • ✅ Cross-checked with official ministry/department websites (.gov.in)
  • ✅ Verified from PIB (Press Information Bureau) press releases
  • ✅ Cross-referenced with at least 2 independent reliable sources
  • ✅ Reviewed by senior editorial team before publishing
  • ✅ Updated immediately when official information changes

We follow a strict Disclaimer Policy and always advise readers to verify important information from official government portals.
