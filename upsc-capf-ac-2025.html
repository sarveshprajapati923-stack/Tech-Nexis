UPSC CAPF AC 2025: 506 पदों पर भर्ती — Assistant Commandant, Eligibility, Exam

Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) ने Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) Assistant Commandant 2025 का notification जारी किया है। इसमें CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, और SSB में 506 पदों पर भर्ती होगी।

📢 CAPF AC 2025: Total Posts: 506 | Apply: upsc.gov.in | Last Date: June 2025 | Exam Date: August 2025 | Qualification: Graduation

Force-wise Vacancy

  • 🔵 BSF (Border Security Force): 165 posts
  • 🟢 CRPF (Central Reserve Police): 142 posts
  • 🟡 CISF (Central Industrial Security): 95 posts
  • ❄️ ITBP (Indo-Tibetan Border Police): 62 posts
  • 🟠 SSB (Sashastra Seema Bal): 42 posts

Eligibility

  • 📚 Education: Bachelor's Degree (any subject) from recognized University
  • 👨 Age: 20 से 25 वर्ष (SC/ST: 5 साल, OBC: 3 साल छूट)
  • 📏 Height (Male): 165 cm | Female: 157 cm
  • 💪 Physical Standards: Fit for arduous duties

Selection Process

  1. 📝 Written Exam (Paper I & II): 450 Marks total — August 2025
  2. 💪 Physical Standard Test (PST)
  3. 💪 Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
  4. 🏥 Medical Examination
  5. 👤 Personality Test / Interview

Written Exam Pattern

  • 📋 Paper I (General Ability & Intelligence): 250 Marks | 2 Hours | MCQ
  • 📋 Paper II (General Studies, Essay & Comprehension): 200 Marks | 3 Hours | Descriptive

Salary

  • 💰 Pay Level 10: ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500 per month
  • 🏠 HRA, DA, Free accommodation, Ration
  • 📈 Promotion: Commandant → DIG → IG

FAQ

Q: CAPF AC के लिए क्या qualification चाहिए?
A: किसी भी stream में Graduation (Bachelor's Degree) किसी भी recognized University से।
Q: CAPF AC की salary कितनी होती है?
A: Pay Level 10 में Basic: ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500। HRA, DA मिलाकर In-hand ₹75,000-₹90,000+।
